# Incident response and learning

Confirm the impact and name one coordinator. Record observations with timestamps,
separate facts from hypotheses, and communicate what is known. Prefer a reversible
mitigation when it restores service faster than a full repair.

After recovery, write a blameless review of contributing conditions and missing
defenses. Choose a small number of corrective actions with owners and verifiable
completion. Avoid "be more careful" as an action. Preserve useful evidence while
redacting credentials and personal information.
